Fatima is a desktop workspace that lets you run, coordinate, and automate teams of specialized AI agents right on your computer. 

---

## What can you do with Fatima?

### 👥 Build Your Custom AI Team
Create specialized agents with distinct personalities, custom instructions, and models. Organize them into collaborative group chats or consult them one-on-one.

### 🛡️ Complete Data Privacy
Fatima runs locally on your machine. Your chats, configurations, databases, and API keys are stored securely on your device and never leave your computer. **No telemetry, tracking, or usage reporting.**

### 🤖 Meet Clotho, Your AI Supervisor
Clotho is your built-in supervisor agent. Pinned to your team, Clotho can monitor your active agents, delegate tasks on your behalf, and auto-reply to routine requests (loop protection included) while escalating complex items to you.

### 📂 Private Knowledge Libraries
Turn local folders, PDFs, and documents into instant knowledge bases. Assign them to specific agents or the whole team so they can refer to your custom files during chats.

### 🔌 Extend with Plugins
Expand your agents' skills. Connect Model Context Protocol (MCP) servers and custom plugins directly from the desktop settings to give agents tools like web search, code runner, and more.

### ⏱️ Automate Daily Routines
Schedule background tasks (cron) to fetch and summarize web content, monitor files, or trigger custom workflows at specific intervals.

### ⚙️ Works with Your Favorite Models
Connect to major cloud providers or run fully offline:
* **Cloud**: OpenRouter, OpenAI, Anthropic Claude, Gemini, DeepSeek.
* **Local/Offline**: Ollama, vLLM, or any custom OpenAI-compatible local endpoint.

## Download & Get Started

Get the latest version of Fatima for your platform from the [Releases](https://github.com/LEMMAIR/fatima-release/releases) page:
* **macOS (Apple Silicon / ARM64)**: `.zip` archive
* **macOS (Intel / x64)**: `.zip` archive
* **Windows (x64)**: `.exe` installer

### Quick Setup
1. Download the package for your operating system.
2. Install and launch the application.
3. On first launch, the onboarding assistant (Clotho) will guide you step-by-step through setting up your preferred AI models and entering your credentials.
